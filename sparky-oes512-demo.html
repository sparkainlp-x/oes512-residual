Sparky — empathetic AI beaver by Fredericton Holograms (frederictonholograms.com)

An independent Spark AI NLP prototype for explainable 512-channel biosignal monitoring. The interface combines a Canadian beaver avatar, grouped channel visualization, transparent anomaly scoring, bilingual messaging, and mandatory human review.

Not a medical device. Uses synthetic or user-supplied non-clinical data only. Human review required for every alert.

Talk to Sparky

Ask a question out loud or type it. Sparky answers from the analysis on screen with simple offline rules (no AI model, no network).

Spoken replies are not available in this browser; replies appear as text.

Personality: Sparky

A personality (.json, up to 64 KB) changes only how Sparky phrases things: name, greeting, empathy, catchphrases and voice. Facts, numbers, alerts and safety answers never change. A loaded file is kept in this browser until you reset.

Privacy: in Chrome (and some other browsers), speech recognition may send your audio to the browser vendor’s servers to be transcribed. Typed questions and all of Sparky’s answers stay on your device.
